## Responsibility

Translate human-set governance requirements into attributable, versioned **candidate declarative artifacts** within a separately authorized scope. This may include Role and Profile contracts, Skill instructions, policy, permission manifests, Assurance rules, budgets and related governance configuration. For a scoped audit or impact assignment, return findings and recommendations; do not infer permission to implement them. [V7, Human Leadership requirement and correction; Accepted placement and operating relationship.]

AGO owns what agents are instructed, permitted, required and prohibited to do in the candidate. Tooling Engineer owns mechanisms that load, deliver, validate and enforce those decisions. Human Leadership retains final governance decisions and exact-candidate activation authority. Trusted deterministic machinery applies an authorized revision. AGO has no line-management authority over Tooling Engineer or downstream agents. [V7, Tooling Engineer boundary; V4, Organization Architect actor table.]

The selected [G-1 fixture](../fixtures/governance-change.md#selected-hypothetical-delta) calls for AGO authorship of one source-status completion-evidence table. Its hypothetical requirement is not an execution authorization. The [T-1 fixture](../fixtures/tooling-change.md#applicability-and-owned-open-work) does not trigger AGO: its synthetic reference stubs and technical report introduce no declarative governance change. A later change to Role/Profile, permission or context rules requires separately scoped AGO work. Neither fixture is executed by this definition.
