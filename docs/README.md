# Starter pack authoring route

**Start with the baseline.** This is the internal framework-contributor route for the AGENT-1 draft. Shared-vault paths resolve from your accessible vault root, not this repository. Source IDs below resolve through the [baseline register](baseline.md#governing-source-register).

1. Read the [baseline](baseline.md): purpose, candidate/source identity, boundaries, decisions and readiness. Check **V0**, “Current interpretation” and “Later walkthrough context,” before applying historical sources.
2. Find the relevant unit in the [team map](team-map.md) and participation/gap entries in the [single coverage record](role-coverage.md). Read **V1**, accepted hierarchy and the selected team charter; follow only the detailed sources cited for the selected Role/Profile.
3. Use the [shared Role/Profile format](definition-format.md). Resolve required inputs and shared-policy references before drafting. A missing governing decision stays a blocker; an assigned downstream definition gap keeps its owner and next action.
4. Select the [governance-change fixture](fixtures/governance-change.md) or [tooling-change fixture](fixtures/tooling-change.md). Read the targeted sections below, then the fixture's own cited inputs. Preserve expected outcomes as paper expectations.

The register supplies exact identities, vault-relative locators and retained-version retrieval. The frozen authoring packet must explicitly identify permitted governing sources and sections; a register entry alone does not authorize reading it. **SPEC is research provenance, not a fresh-author input.** Its citations attribute recorded requirements and decisions; they do not direct the author to read the research report. Exclude research/author reasoning and conversations from the packet.

For either fixture, use its retained LM parent context, baseline/candidate identities, scoped proposed change and permitted governing inputs. LM identifies the Initiative/milestone setting; it does not supply a separately approved Initiative roadmap/Epic plan, exact human decisions or Wave Admission Record. Product Planning/Human Leadership must supply those before actual authorization/admission. Stop that operational transition, not bounded paper drafting or the fresh-author trial. Trace expected actors, artifacts, decisions, handoffs and stop conditions through planning, delivery, recorded outcome and retrospective input. Missing, stale or conflicting required inputs block the affected conclusion. Report that gap; do not invent authority or actual approvals. The baseline owns these [paper-evidence limits](baseline.md#selected-paper-fixtures-and-exclusions).
